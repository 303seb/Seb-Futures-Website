House rules

How we keep it worth being in

Short list, strictly enforced. It's the reason the server stays useful.

Do

  • Show your work — post charts with your reasoning, not just outcomes
  • Ask questions — no such thing as too basic in here
  • Journal honestly — including the trades you'd rather forget
  • Help newer members — teaching it is how you learn it
  • Respect the room — disagree with the idea, not the person

Don't

  • No signal selling — or promoting your own paid group
  • No DM soliciting — anyone DMing you an offer is a scammer, report it
  • No fake P&L — doctored screenshots are an instant ban
  • No account management — never send anyone money to trade for you
  • No hype — this isn't a room for "easy money" talk

Important

I will never DM you first asking for money, offering to trade your account, or promising returns. Anyone who does is impersonating me — block, report, and let a moderator know. Everything official is announced in the server's announcement channels only.
