Who I am

Sebastian Salazar

I trade NQ and ES futures during the New York session. I started the way most people do — jumping between indicators, blowing accounts, and blaming the market. What changed things was narrowing down to one instrument, one session, and one setup, and journaling it until it was boring.

Now I share that process publicly. Everything I teach is what I'm actually doing on my own charts, posted live — including the trades that don't work.
