The fine print

Rules & eligibility

Eligibility

  • 18 or older at the time of entry
  • Active Discord member before the giveaway closes
  • One entry per person — alt accounts are disqualified
  • Void where prohibited by local law
  • Previous winners must wait 90 days to re-enter

How winners are picked

  • Random draw from all verified entries
  • Announced publicly in the giveaway channel
  • 7 days to claim before it gets redrawn
  • Prizes aren't transferable or exchangeable for cash
  • Contacted in-server only — never by unsolicited DM

Scam warning

You will never be asked to pay a fee, send crypto, or provide banking details to claim a prize. I do not DM winners first — all winners are announced publicly in the server and claim through the official channel. If someone contacts you claiming you've won and asks for money or credentials, it is a scam. Block them and report it to a moderator.

Terms

No purchase necessary to enter or win. Giveaways are run independently and are not sponsored, endorsed or administered by Discord, X, Instagram, YouTube, or any prop firm unless explicitly stated in the giveaway post. Prizes covering evaluation fees do not guarantee passing an evaluation or receiving a payout — those outcomes depend entirely on your own trading and the firm's own terms. Full terms are posted alongside each giveaway in the server.
