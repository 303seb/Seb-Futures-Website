Trading & strategy

Index futures only — primarily NQ (Nasdaq 100 E-mini) and ES (S&P 500 E-mini), plus their micro equivalents MNQ and MES.

I don't trade crypto, forex or options, and I don't teach them. Narrowing down to two correlated instruments in one session is a large part of why the process works.

The New York session. Plans are posted around 8:00 AM ET, the live session runs through the 9:30 AM open, and I'll sometimes take an afternoon setup. Full schedule is on the Discord page.

If you're in another timezone, the recaps and breakdowns are all recorded and posted so you can work through them whenever.

TradingView for charting and analysis, and a futures broker or prop platform for execution. No paid indicators, no black-box tools — just price, sessions, and levels.
